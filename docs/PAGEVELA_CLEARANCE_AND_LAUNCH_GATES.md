**Trademark assessment framework (USPTO):** [Likelihood of confusion](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/likelihood-confusion) evaluates **overall similarity of the complete marks** (sound, appearance, meaning and commercial impression) **and relatedness of actual goods/services**. Class overlap or sharing four letters is **not** proof of confusion, and differing classes are not conclusive proof of safety. See also the [UKIPO similar-goods/services guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/searching-for-similar-trade-mark-goodsservices-in-other-classes).

**Source quality:** OPENVELA serial **99084408** is an indexed USPTO search result; **98766400** is from a **secondary Justia mirror** pending current-status check on USPTO TSDR. XIAOMI VELA is a US government TTAB proceeding; the two standalone VELA entries above are also **secondary aggregators pending authoritative verification**. No lawyer clearance or current-domain checkout has been performed. This inquiry is a **non-exhaustive triage log**, not a conclusion that Pagevela is free for public use.
